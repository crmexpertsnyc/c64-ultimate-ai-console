⏻ Power on your C64 Ultimate from the app

The C64 Ultimate can't be woken over the network once it's off. A small smart plug fixes that: the app switches the plug, and the C64 starts when its power comes back. It takes about 15 minutes to set up.

🔌 Wall outlet↓ 📶 Smart plug — the app switches it over your home Wi-Fi↓ C64 Ultimate power supply↓ 🖥 C64 Ultimate — its own power switch left ON

1. First, check that your C64 Ultimate starts by itself

Leave the C64 Ultimate's own power switch ON, unplug its power supply from the wall, wait a few seconds, and plug it back in.

Does it start without touching it? Then a smart plug will work. If it stays off until you press its switch, a smart plug can only cut its power (⏻ Power off still works from the app), not start it.

2. Get a plug that works without the cloud

The app talks to the plug directly on your network, so it needs a plug with a local control API. These work:

3. Set it up

  1. Plug it in and add it to your Wi-Fi with the maker's phone app (Shelly Smart Control, or Tasmota's own Wi-Fi setup page). Use the same network as the console.
  2. Find the plug's IP address. In the Shelly app: device → Settings → Device information. Or look in your router's list of connected devices. It looks like 192.168.1.50.
  3. Keep that address fixed. In your router, reserve the address for the plug ("DHCP reservation" / "static lease"), so it doesn't change after a power cut.
  4. Leave local access open. Don't switch on login protection for the plug's local web page (Shelly: Settings → Authentication off) — the app doesn't use a plug password.
  5. Check it from a browser on the same network: open http://192.168.1.50 (your plug's address). You should see the plug's own page.
  6. Plug the C64 Ultimate's power supply into the smart plug and switch the C64's own power switch ON — and leave it on from now on.
  7. Tell the app: open the console's Settings → C64 Ultimate → ⏻ Smart plug, choose the plug type from the table, enter the plug's IP address, and press Save changes.
  8. Try it: on the C64 Screen page press ⏻ Power off (the C64 turns off, then the plug), then ⏻ Power on — the C64 Ultimate is back in about 10 seconds.
What the buttons do
  • ⏻ Power off — asks the C64 Ultimate to shut down properly, then switches the plug off a few seconds later.
  • ⏻ Power on — switches the plug off for 3 seconds, then on, so the C64 Ultimate starts fresh.

Troubleshooting

"The smart plug didn't answer"Check the IP address in Settings, and that http://<plug IP> opens in a browser on the same network. The plug must not have login protection switched on.
The plug clicks but the C64 stays offThe C64 Ultimate's own power switch must be left ON. Repeat the test in step 1.
It worked, then stoppedThe plug probably got a new IP address after a router restart — reserve it (step 3) and update Settings.
Using the app away from homeWorks the same over your remote access (e.g. Tailscale): the console at home talks to the plug.

Technical details: Shelly Gen1 — /relay/0?turn=on|off; Shelly Plus/Gen2–Gen4 — Switch.Set; Tasmota — Power On|Off. The app only ever contacts the plug address you enter, and only when you press a power button.
